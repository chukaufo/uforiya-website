The process

From upload to collaboration in minutes

01

Upload your audio and start a project

Drop in your file and Uforiya handles the rest. BPM detection, key analysis, instant organisation. Structure your projects your way: single, album, EP, mixtape or playlist.

02

Invite your team

Add collaborators by username or email and give each person the right role. Editors can upload and own their tracks. Everyone works in the same space. No files passed around.

03

Version as you go

Upload new versions at every stage, from rough idea to final record. Only the track owner marks a version as current, so nothing changes without the right sign-off.

04

Build together

Stream tracks, leave timestamped comments, watch the project grow. Your whole creative process in one place. No more chasing files, no more losing the take that sounded right.
