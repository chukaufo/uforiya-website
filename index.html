Everything the session is missing

Files spread across message threads and shared drives are how records get lost. Uforiya keeps the music, the history and the people in one place.

Track management

Upload your audio and get BPM and key detection on the way in. Organise tracks into singles, EPs, albums, mixtapes or playlists.

Version history

Every revision stays exactly where you left it. Return to any version at any time and keep a clear record of how the track got here.

Collaborator roles

Invite people by username or email and set what each person can do. Owners, editors and listeners get exactly the access they need.

Access control

Keep a project private, open it to specific collaborators, or make it public. You decide who hears the record and when they hear it.

Player and comments

Stream in the browser with waveform playback, leave comments pinned to a timestamp, and upload stems alongside every version.

Play data

See which records are getting attention inside your circle before they ever reach a streaming platform.

The process

Four steps from upload to release

  1. 01

    Start the project

    Drop in your file and the analysis runs on upload. BPM, key and duration are read automatically, then the track lands in a project you structure your way.

  2. 02

    Bring in your people

    Add collaborators by username or email and assign the role that fits. Editors upload and own their own tracks. Everyone works in the same space instead of passing files around.

  3. 03

    Version as the record grows

    Upload a new version at every stage, from the first rough idea through to the final master. Only the track owner marks a version as current, so nothing changes without the right sign off.

  4. 04

    Take it to release

    When the record is finished, push it out to streaming platforms without leaving Uforiya. Distribution arrives with the beta release.
