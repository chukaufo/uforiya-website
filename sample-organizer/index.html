The problem

Kits repeat each other, and filenames never tell you

Buy enough drum kits and the same sounds start showing up in several of them, renamed every time. One kit turns out to contain another kit whole. You cannot find that by reading folder names, and no producer is going to listen through 5,000 one-shots to check.

Matches contents, not names

Two files are duplicates when their bytes are identical, whatever they are called. Uzi 808.wav and 808 [Spinz].wav can be the same sound, and this catches it.

Exact only, never similar

No similarity scoring and no thresholds. A "97% match" is the one thing you cannot act on when the decision is whether to delete a sound.

Fast, then instant

A full first scan of 4,800 samples takes about a second. After that it only looks at what changed, so reopening it costs nothing.

Stays on your machine

No account, no upload, no server. Your library is never sent anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to be sent.

Safety

Nothing is deleted without two deliberate actions

Removing a sample you cannot get back is worse than keeping a duplicate forever, so the app is built to make that impossible by accident.

  1. 01

    One copy of everything always survives

    Any selection that would empty a group is refused outright, and the refusal names the file. That check runs in the engine, not the interface, so no click can get around it.

  2. 02

    Listen before you decide

    Every copy has a play button next to it. A byte-identical match is only reassuring once you have heard that both files are the sound you think they are.

  3. 03

    Removals are staged, not deleted

    Approved files move to a holding area on your disk. They have left your library, they are still on your machine, and one button puts every one of them back.

  4. 04

    Permanent deletion is its own decision

    It only happens when you choose it, on a batch you name. No timer, no cleanup on launch, no side effect of anything else.

Questions

Before you install it

Does it need a Uforiya account?

No. It has no account, no login and no connection to the Uforiya platform at all. It is a separate desktop app that happens to come from the same place.

Does my library get uploaded anywhere?

Never. Every scan, every comparison and every file move happens on your own machine. The app works with your internet off.

Can it delete something I wanted to keep?

Removals go to a holding area first, not to the bin, and one copy of every sample is always kept no matter what you select. Permanent deletion is a separate action on a batch you choose.

What counts as a duplicate?

Identical file contents. Not similar sounds, not similar names — the same bytes. If two files differ at all, they are treated as different samples.

Which platforms?

Windows and macOS at launch, Linux after. It is being tested with producers now.

What does it cost?

Nothing.
