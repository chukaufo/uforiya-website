What is Uforiya

Uforiya is the collaboration platform built for music creatives. Whether you're an artist, producer, songwriter, or engineer — Uforiya gives your whole team one place to work together without losing control of your music.

Invite collaborators to specific projects or individual tracks. Assign roles, manage permissions, and decide exactly who hears what. Your music stays yours.

Every track supports multiple versions so your team can iterate freely — upload a new mix, compare versions, and always know which one is current.

Organize everything into projects — albums, EPs, singles, whatever you're working on. Your whole creative process in one place, not scattered across iMessage and Google Drive.

Upload your in-progress tracks through web or mobile and listen to your unreleased work anytime, anywhere.

Our Vision

Uforiya is being built to become the essential workspace for independent music creators.

We believe the best music is made together. Uforiya exists to make that collaboration seamless, secure, and built around how artists actually work.

This is just the beginning. More tools, more features, and more ways to support your creative process are on the way.
