What is Uforiya

Uforiya is a cloud hard drive for music creatives. It helps you manage your creative process without things getting disorganized.

The app lets you upload your in-progress tracks through web or mobile and listen to your unreleased work anytime.

Each track can have up to five versions, so you can manage different ideas and track how your music evolves.

You can also create projects and add tracks to them like playlists. This makes it easier to organize albums, EPs, or anything you're working on.

Collaboration is a big part of Uforiya. You can add collaborators to tracks or projects and control their roles and permissions, so you stay in control while working with others.

Users can upload up to 25 tracks for now, with plans to introduce a Pro tier with higher limits and more features.

Our Vision

Uforiya is being built to become an essential platform for independent music creators.

The goal is to create something that supports artists as they grow, collaborate, and build their careers over time.
